## Finding
  The dashboard's payment p95 of 8-10 s (C2-3) comes from the payment gateway call, not from
  Robot Shop: the GET to https://paypal.com/ (a real external site; upstream calls it as a dummy
  gateway) takes a median 95% of each payment request. The internal calls (user, cart, RabbitMQ)
  take 1-10 ms each.

  capstone's own. Only payment is instrumented (the brief asks for one instrumented service).

  pinned OpenTelemetry packages (1.41.1 / 0.62b1 are the last releases for Python 3.9).
- Jaeger was first installed at the chart's 2.22.0. Grafana's Jaeger datasource then failed its
  health check with '404 Not Found': Jaeger 2.21 removed the old /api/services and /api/traces
  search endpoints that Grafana 13.2's plugin still calls (grafana/grafana-jaeger-datasource#87).
  Pinned to 2.20.0 (jaeger rev 2); health check, service list and search work. Traces are kept
  in memory, so the restart cleared the earlier ones (e.g. 3449c809..., 1,904 ms total with
  1,800 ms in the paypal.com call).

- 'minikube image build' re-downloaded the 360 MB base image into BuildKit's own cache.
